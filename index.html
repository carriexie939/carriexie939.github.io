HomeLM, AI property search

Product Manager · Apr 2025 – Jul 2026
Problem
Home buyers looking at new developments and off-the-plan projects struggle to turn what they want into a property search.
My role
Owned the roadmap, with objectives set every two weeks, and four live features: AI adviser, map overlays, listing cards and investment summaries. Shaped search, personalised recommendations and nearby-amenity content, built a persona-based LLM evaluation (50+ queries), and proposed the monetisation plan, including pricing and packaging, which investors endorsed.
Key decision
Users told us the one-page homepage felt like a scam. Leadership wanted to keep it simple, so I proposed an A/B test and ran user tests. A second page lifted click-through rate and shipped. On search, I argued for shipping a fast, roughly 80%-accurate first version and improving precision in phases.
Also
Led an AI upgrade of the agents' CRM. When adoption was low, I asked agents about their real workflow and redesigned it around follow-up scenarios, status tags and reminders. The sales team rolled it out to agents, who now write less and follow up faster.

AI Job Search System

Notion · Codex · MCP · built in 3 days
Problem
Finding the right roles and tailoring for each one by hand was slow, and generic resumes got ignored.
Built
A human-in-the-loop workflow: AI localises my resume for Australia, finds recent high-match roles on Seek, Indeed and LinkedIn, scores each one and logs every application.
Learned
Fit scores alone predicted outcomes weakly, so I added gap analysis and manual review. It now screens about 10 roles a day, with 80+ tracked.
